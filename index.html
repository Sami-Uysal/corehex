CORE.HEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Developer: Sami Uysal

Data collected

CORE.HEX does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email or location. Game progress is stored only on your device.

The app uses these third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — may process the device Advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to serve ads. Users in the EEA/UK are asked for consent to personalised ads; you can change this via "Ad Privacy" in the shop screen. Google ads policy
  • Google Play Games Services — uses your Play Games player id for leaderboards and achievements. Google privacy policy
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat — process purchase records and an anonymous user id to handle in-app purchases. Payment details never reach us. RevenueCat privacy policy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Uninstalling the app deletes local game data.
